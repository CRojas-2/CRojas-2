## Featured projects

### [Fintech Loan Portfolio: From Data to Credit Decisions](https://github.com/CRojas-2/fintech-loan-portfolio-analysis)
`BigQuery SQL` `Python` `SciPy` `statsmodels` `Data Studio (Looker Studio)` `Git`

**The question:** a fintech with 270,292 personal loans. Does the portfolio make money, and where is it losing it?

**What I found:** it's profitable, but barely. The margin is $17.86M, only 6% of the interest collected, and one more point of default would cost $14.8M. Price doesn't follow risk: grade G charges 29% and defaults 46%. And 80% of the money in delinquency sits in 13 of 28 segments.

**What it supports:** reprice from grade C, and pilot an approval rule on payment-to-income. Rejecting applicants above 15% adds $5.9M while turning away only 6.1% of loans.

### [RappiPlus: From Data to Business Decisions](https://github.com/CRojas-2/rappiplus-analytics-project)
`Python` `PostgreSQL` `statsmodels` `Power BI`

**The question:** a delivery subscription service in Colombia, Mexico and Argentina. Is it doing well, and where should it act first?

**What I found:** $51.8M in revenue with an 11.35% margin, but Mexico's paid search channel loses $521K. Checkout is the biggest leak in the funnel: 958 users drop between starting checkout and adding a payment method.

**What it supports:** audit that channel's costs and pricing, fix the payment step first, and don't ship the tested checkout redesign. Its A/B test showed no real effect (p = 0.42).

## What I bring

- **Clean data before clever charts.** Reusable Python and SQL checks for nulls, duplicates, inconsistent categories, broken keys and outliers.
- **Models that hold up.** Relational models (star, snowflake, fact constellation, bridge tables) so the same number shows up the same way in every filter.
- **Statistics with a purpose.** A/B tests, z-tests, chi-square, Cramér's V. I use them to decide what to act on and, just as often, what to rule out.
- **Dashboards that explain themselves.** Every chart carries one sentence saying what it shows, built in Power BI, Tableau or Data Studio.
- **Automation where it saves real time.** At ARN, the Python cleanup freed about 3 hours a week (roughly 150 hours a year), and an AI-assisted outreach app handled daily contact with 60 to 80 people.
